Conclusion

SAG is a conditional improvement, not a faster sort for everything.

At least 10% faster on low-entropy data with long or medium keys: 12 of 16 cells.

No severe slowdown in 50 cells. Every output correct and stable.

Short keys gain little. The margin is narrow (12 against 11 needed).

Also done: a comparator-exception bug fixed; SAG-C rejected.

Limits: mostly synthetic data, one machine and compiler, internal audit.
