Streak-Adaptive Galloping for Natural Merge Sort

2 / 13

Sorting is used everywhere. The merge step spends comparisons. When keys are long strings, one comparison costs 17 to 70 nanoseconds in our measurements, against about 1 ns for integers, so comparisons dominate the time. The base paper gives a safe rule for the merge step but it does not adapt to the data. Our question: can it adapt, and does saving comparisons really save time?
