Streak-Adaptive Galloping for Natural Merge Sort

Making the merge step of Merge Sort spend fewer comparisons, and checking when that really saves time.

Base paper: Ghasemi, Jugé, Khalighinejad, Yazdanyar · Algorithmica 87(2), 2025

Two sorted runs are merged into one sorted list.

Introduce the project: we re-implemented a 2024 journal paper on the merge step of natural merge sorts, added one idea called SAG, and tested when it really helps.
