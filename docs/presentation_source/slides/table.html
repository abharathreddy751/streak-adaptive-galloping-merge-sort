The pattern is clear: the gain follows how expensive one comparison is. Long keys gain the most, medium keys less, short keys almost nothing, and nearly sorted data gains in every key type. Random order is neutral. Two small real file-name lists were also tested: 4 percent faster in natural order, no change when shuffled; they are too small to prove a practical benefit.
