Streak-Adaptive Galloping for Natural Merge Sort

5 / 13

Textbook Merge Sort is n log n. A natural merge sort needs only n minus 1 comparisons on sorted input. The paper proves n plus n times H-star comparisons for its rule, where H-star measures how mixed the data is. Galloping a streak of m items costs m when m is at most t plus 2, otherwise t plus 2 times the log of m minus t. Our SAG pays at most four thirds of the streak length per streak, checked on a finite range, not proven.
